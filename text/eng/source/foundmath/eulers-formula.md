You may have heard of Euler's number: $\mathrm{e} \approx 2.717\cdots$.

## Polar Form

$$ \begin{align*}
    r\mathrm{e}^{\theta\mathrm{i}} = r(\cos(\theta) + \sin(\theta)\mathrm{i})
\end{align*} $$

## Euler's Identity

$$ \begin{align*}
    \mathrm{e}^{\pi\mathrm{i}} + 1 = 0
\end{align*} $$
